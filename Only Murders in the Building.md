# Only Murders in the Building

One show I watched this year is called __Only Murders in the Building__, which is a comedy mystery show that takes place in a luxury apartment building in New York City. It stars Steve Martin, Martin Short, and Selena Gomez as residents of the apartment that solve a different murder that takes place in their apartment building every season. It riffs on true crime podcasts and features many big-name stars in guest roles, such as Meryl Streep, Nathan Lane, and Paul Rudd. This show features silly murder mysteries, but is very cozy and is best watched curled up on a couch under a blanket with low lighting. The title credits allude to this as well.
